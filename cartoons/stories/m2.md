"Pass the cumin," Maya said, not looking up from the sizzling skillet.

Leo squinted at the spice rack. "Define 'cumin.'"

"Small jar. Brown powder. Smells like tacos."

"They all smell like dust and ambition." He grabbed a jar. "Paprika?"

"No. That’s red. Look for a C."

"Coriander?"

"Keep going."

"Cardamom?"

"Leo, the onions are caramelizing into charcoal."

"Found it! Cumin!" He tossed it over. Maya caught it one-handed, unscrewed the cap, and shook a generous cloud into the pan. A sharp, earthy aroma instantly hit the ceiling.

"See? Magic," she said, stirring vigorously. "Now taste this."

She held out a wooden spoon. Leo leaned in, blew on it twice, and took a sip. His eyebrows shot up. "Whoa. Okay. That actually tastes like real food."

"You sound surprised."

"I am. Twenty minutes ago, this was a sad potato and an existential crisis."

Maya grinned, turning off the burner. "Grab the bowls. Dinner's ready."
