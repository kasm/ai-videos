# The shy visitor

One sunny morning Pip and Mo stand in the meadow. Mo sighs and says that
nothing ever happens here.

Just then somebody new walks in from the left: a small green fellow called
Tuk, who has come over the hills. Pip sees him first, waves and calls
hello. Tuk is shy. He stops a little way off, says a quiet hello, and turns
away.

Mo points at him and asks where he comes from. Tuk turns back. He says he
comes from far away, behind the hills, and that he is looking for a place
to sleep, because the night is near.

The sun goes down. Pip tells Tuk he can stay with them: the meadow is big
enough for three. Tuk is so glad that he jumps three times.

Then the night falls and the moon comes up. The three friends wave good
night to each other. And Mo was wrong: something did happen today.
