A story to its videos

In the project {{project}}, on this machine alone.

1. The story

Lowercase letters, digits and dashes. The text is saved as stories/<name>.md, and its episodes are named after it.

2. Its prompt

The story, wrapped in what a web chat needs to write its episodes.

3. The chat's answer

Paste the prompt into a new chat, ChatGPT or any other. When it has answered, copy its whole answer with its copy button, which keeps the ```yaml blocks, and paste it here. An episode copied by its own block's button, with no fence, is taken too.

Adds it to stories/<name>.reply.md, and imports its episodes. With the field empty, it goes on from that file. When the chat has written every episode and none is refused, it makes them.

Goes on with the episodes taken so far: for a chat that never says it is done, or an episode it cannot mend.
